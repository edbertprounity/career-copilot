**Rules as built**: Entries (low): the job skills are the CV's Skills and Technologies items that the profile supports and the job description mentions (stemmed whole-phrase match). A Projects or Experience entry naming none of them is flagged. With no job skills at all, nothing is flagged. Requirements: `job["requirements"]` (a list from Area 2's parser) is used when present. Otherwise the blocks after Requirements / Minimum qualifications are required (medium) and those after Preferred / Desirable / Nice to have, including "Desirable experience includes" without a colon, are preferred (low). Responsibilities and About the role are ignored; with no such header, the whole description is required. Clauses split on `;`, line breaks and sentence ends. Clauses naming degree, bachelor, master, PhD, doctorate, postgraduate, year(s) or qualification(s) are dropped. Items split on commas, and lead-ins such as "strong knowledge of" or "experience with" are stripped, so every quote stays a verbatim substring. An item is addressed if any content word (stemmed; generic words such as experience, knowledge, skills, ability, strong, working, familiarity, proficiency, good, basic and solid excluded; at least 3 letters or a 2-letter acronym) appears anywhere in the CV. At most 5 requirement issues, required first, in job order.
